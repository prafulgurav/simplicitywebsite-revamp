What's new in Indian investing

The shifts worth knowing about

We track regulation and product innovation so you don't have to. Here's what matters right now.

Regulation

Specialized Investment Funds (SIFs)

A SEBI-created category with a ₹10 lakh minimum that allows strategies such as long-short equity — a bridge between mutual funds and PMS.

Global

GIFT City & global diversification

IFSC-domiciled funds are opening cleaner routes to global markets for resident Indians (via LRS) and NRIs.

Behaviour

Step-up SIPs

Raising your SIP with your salary each year often matters more than picking the "best" fund. Try it in the planner above.

Data

Account Aggregator

RBI's consent-based data-sharing framework makes a complete, real-time view of your finances possible — with your permission.

Legacy

Nominations & unclaimed money

Tighter nomination rules exist for a reason — thousands of crores sit unclaimed. We make sure yours never will.

Safety

Fraud-proof investing

Fake "investment advisory" groups are on the rise. We never ask for OTPs or passwords, and never promise returns.

FAQ

Straight answers

How are you paid?

We are an AMFI-registered mutual fund distributor (ARN-87401). We are paid commissions by the asset management companies, which are included in the expense ratio of regular plans. We disclose these to you, as required by SEBI.

What is the minimum amount to start?

You can start a SIP from as little as ₹500 a month in most funds. Specialized Investment Funds need a minimum of ₹10 lakh across SIFs of a fund house.

Do you work with NRIs?

Yes. We help NRIs with KYC, FATCA declarations, NRE/NRO routing and choosing fund houses that accept investments from their country of residence.

Is my money held by you?

No. Your investments are held in your own name with the fund house and its registrar (CAMS/KFintech). Payments go directly to the fund house — never to us.

How often will we review my portfolio?

At least once a year, plus whenever your life changes — a new job, a child, a move abroad — or when your asset mix drifts too far from plan.
