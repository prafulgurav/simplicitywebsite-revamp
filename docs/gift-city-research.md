# GIFT City research: what to add to the Simplicity Investing website

_Research date: 30 Sep 2026. Tax and regulatory points must be checked by a CA and compliance before anything is published._

## 1. Why now

- **Domestic international funds have mostly closed.** SEBI caps the mutual fund industry's overseas investment at $7 bn. As of 10 Jul 2026, only one Indian international fund (Baroda BNP Paribas Aqua FoF) was open for fresh SIPs. [Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/invest/heres-why-indian-investors-face-a-shrinking-international-fund-menu-even-as-global-markets-rally), [Value Research](https://www.valueresearchonline.com/stories/228698/international-funds-are-shutting-down-these-12-still-take-your-sip/)
- **GIFT City funds are outside that cap.** IFSCA regulates them, not SEBI. Residents invest through LRS (up to $250k per financial year). [Anand Rathi](https://anandrathi.com/blog/why-investment-in-international-mutual-funds-stopped-in-india)
- **The market is growing fast:** about 300 funds from 180+ fund managers by Feb 2026. The IFSCA Fund Management Regulations 2025 lowered the minimum scheme corpus to $3M. [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/india/fund-management-reits/1784396/ifsca-tightens-fund-management-regime-in-gift-city-accountability-certification-and-governance), [Enterslice](https://enterslice.com/learning/ifsca-fund-management-regulation/)
- **NSE IX Global Access** went live in late Feb 2026. It offers US stocks with fractional shares, and 30+ more markets are planned. [5paisa](https://www.5paisa.com/news/nse-ix-launches-us-stock-access-under-global-access-platform), [HDFC Sky](https://hdfcsky.com/news/nse-ix-enables-direct-investment-in-us-stocks)

## 2. Can Simplicity (an AMFI distributor) offer GIFT City products?

**Yes.** There are two routes ([Cafemutual](https://cafemutual.com/news/askus/32906-mfds-can-distribute-ifsc-products-without-setting-up-office-in-gift-city), [Cafemutual](https://cafemutual.com/news/industry/32308-opportunities-for-mfds-in-gift-city), [GiftCFO](https://www.giftcfo.com/post/navigating-ifsca-s-informal-guidance-on-distributor-eligibility-and-dual-registration-in-gift-city)):

| Route | Requirements | Fit |
|---|---|---|
| **Empanelment** with IFSC fund houses / PMS / AIF managers | No exam, no net-worth rule, no GIFT City office. The distributor code of conduct still applies | **Start here** |
| **IFSCA distributor registration** | Office in GIFT City, $50k net worth, fees of $750 (application) + $2,000 (registration) + $2,000 per year | Later, once volumes justify it |

## 4. Tax and cost points to explain (CA to verify)

- **TCS:** 20% on LRS remittances above ₹10 lakh in a financial year. It can be claimed back as credit in the tax return ([DSP GIFT City](https://giftcity.dspim.com/knowledge-hub/gift-city-fund-taxation)).
- **Outbound fund taxes (DSP example):** the fund pays tax at the trust level (14.95% long-term after 24 months, 42.74% short-term). The investor has no capital gains tax on redemption and no TDS ([DSP GIFT City](https://giftcity.dspim.com/knowledge-hub/gift-city-fund-taxation)).
- **US estate tax:** holding US stocks directly exposes a non-US person to US estate tax above $60k (up to 40%). A GIFT City fund of funds holding non-US (UCITS) funds avoids this. UDRs probably do not ([Rovia](https://www.rovia.one/resources/taxation/gift-city-vs-us-brokerage), [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/finance/personal-finance/us-inheritance-tax-threatens-hni-investors-can-gift-city-be-a-solution-124062000119_1.html)).
- **NRI inbound funds:** marketed as having no Indian capital gains tax or TDS, and certain retail schemes need no PAN or Indian tax return (Sec 10(4D)). Sources disagree on the details, so verify before publishing.

## 6. What to add to the site

1. **"Global" hub page** leading with: *"International funds closed for SIPs? There's still a regulated route."*
2. **Route finder:** "Resident or NRI?" plus goal and amount, leading to a recommended route (GIFT outbound fund / NSE IX / USD FD / domestic international fund if open).
3. **Route comparison table:** GIFT City fund vs direct US broker vs domestic international fund, covering minimums, SIP availability, TCS, tax, estate-tax exposure and currency.
4. **LRS and TCS calculator:** amount already remitted this year, then remaining headroom out of $250k and the TCS that will be collected (and later credited).
5. **Fund shelf:** filter by eligibility (Resident/NRI), minimum, currency and strategy; a factsheet link for each fund.
6. **Onboarding tracker:** KYC, IFSC account, LRS remittance and courier form. Many fund houses still need signed physical forms within 15–20 days ([Cafemutual](https://cafemutual.com/news/industry/32308-opportunities-for-mfds-in-gift-city)).
7. **GIFT Nifty live widget** and a GIFT City FAQ with schema markup, so the pages show up in search and AI answers.
8. **Compliance block:** IFSCA-regulated (not SEBI), currency risk, LRS limits, and the empanelment disclosure.

## 7. Out-of-the-box idea: the "Global Indian Family" dashboard

One USD/INR view for families split across countries: Indian mutual funds, GIFT City funds, USD FDs and NRE/NRO balances. Add a **Returning-to-India (RNOR) planner** that shows what to redeem, move or hold as residency changes. Few platforms serve this household today.

## 8. Risks and challenges

- **High minimums** ($500–$5,000) plus TCS on cash flow make this a product for affluent clients, not small SIP investors.
- **Onboarding still involves paper.** Budget for concierge operations.
- **Regulation is moving.** Tax parity debates and questions about NSE IX rupee outflows ([Moneylife](https://www.moneylife.in/article/nse-ix-opens-direct-us-stock-trading-for-indians-sparks-regulatory-debate/79796.html)). Date-stamp all content.
- **Avoid "tax-free" hype** and anything that sounds like investment advice. Stay within the distributor code of conduct.
