    FAQ

    Straight answers

    How are you paid?

    We are an AMFI-registered mutual fund distributor (ARN-87401). Fund houses pay us commission, which is included in the expense ratio of regular plans. We disclose it to you as SEBI requires.

    Can a resident Indian invest in GIFT City funds?

    Yes, in outbound funds that invest abroad, through the RBI's Liberalised Remittance Scheme (up to $250,000 per financial year). Funds that invest into India from GIFT City are for non-residents only.

    Is the 20% TCS an extra tax?

    No. It is collected by your bank on investment remittances above ₹10 lakh in a financial year and shows in your Form 26AS. You claim it as credit when you file your return. It does affect your cash flow until then.

    Does Saral AI give investment advice?

    No. Saral explains your portfolio, the likely effect of events, and the options in front of you, and shows the data behind each answer. It never moves money. Any action is discussed and confirmed with a Simplicity expert, in line with SEBI's proposed rules on how AI may be used with clients.

    Which tax regime should I choose?

    For most salaried people, the new regime now costs less unless their deductions are large (home loan interest, HRA, 80C, NPS). Use the tax planner above to see where you break even, then we can confirm it with your actual figures.

    Do you work with NRIs?

    Yes. We help with KYC, FATCA declarations, NRE/NRO routing, GIFT City USD funds and fund houses that accept investors from your country.

    Do you hold my money?

    No. Investments are held in your name with the fund house and its registrar. Payments go directly to the fund house.
